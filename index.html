Cashbook & Journal Challenge Round 1

Triple-column cash book & General Journal for credit asset purchases

Pearson IAL Cash Book & Journal Key Rules:
  • Triple Column Cash Book: Debit side contains Discount Allowed, Cash Dr, Bank Dr. Credit side contains Discount Received, Cash Cr, Bank Cr.
  • Contra Entries: "Banked cash in hand" = Dr Bank, Cr Cash (Ref "C"). "Withdrew cash for business use" = Dr Cash, Cr Bank.
  • General Journal: Used ONLY for non-current long-term asset purchases on credit. Format: Asset Dr, Supplier Cr.
