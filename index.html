Cashbook & Journal Challenge Round 1

Triple-column cash book (Cash, Bank, Discount) & General Journal for credit asset purchases

Pearson IAL Cash Book & Journal Key Rules:
  • Triple Column Cash Book: Debit side contains Discount Allowed, Cash Dr, Bank Dr. Credit side contains Discount Received, Cash Cr, Bank Cr.
  • Contra Entries: "Banked cash in hand" = Dr Bank, Cr Cash (Reference "C"). "Withdrew cash for business use" = Dr Cash, Cr Bank.
  • Cheque Payments with Settlements: The actual cheque amount goes into Bank, while settlement savings go into Discount Received or Discount Allowed columns!
  • General Journal: Used ONLY for credit purchases of non-current long term assets (e.g., machinery, delivery vehicles). Format: Asset Dr, Supplier Cr.
